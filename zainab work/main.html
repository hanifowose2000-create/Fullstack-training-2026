VANILLA CAKE

A classic vanilla cake recipe that is perfect for any occasion. Moist, fluffy, and full of vanilla flavor.

Ingredient

  • 250gram of butter
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 2 cups of flour
  • 5 large eggs
  • 5 ml baking powder
  • 1/8 tsp vanilla flavour
  • 1/8 tsp preservative
  • 1/8 tsp milk flavour
  • 1/8 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/8 tsp cinnamon powder

Methodology

  1. Prepare your pan (grease and dust with flour)
  2. Mix your butter and sugar till it light and fluffy
  3. Separate your yolk from your egg white in a bowl
  4. In a bowl sieve your flour and all your dry ingredients
  5. Whisk your egg white till it foam
  6. Pour your egg yolk into your butter and sugar mixture and mix
  7. Put your flavour into the butter and sugar and egg mixture
  8. Start with whisked egg and end with whisk egg when mixing the butter and sugar mixture with the dry ingredients
  9. Pour your egg white and mix your flour and mix a bit
  10. Mix together till there is no lump of flour
  11. Pour the mixture into the prepared pan
  12. In other note preheat your oven 5 minutes before you put your cake in the oven
  13. Put your cake in the oven
  14. To check if your cake is done, insert a toothpick into the center. If it comes out clean, your cake is done.
    NOTES:
  • Make sure to measure your ingredients accurately.
  • Do not overmix the batter.
  • Do not overbake the cake.
  • Don't open the oven door while the cake is baking.
  • Don't cover hot cake

DOUGHTNUT

A classic vanilla cake recipe that is perfect for any occasion. Moist, fluffy, and full of vanilla flavor.

Ingredient

  • 50gram of butter
  • 100gram of sugar
  • 500gram of flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 tsp of salt
  • 1 tbsp of yeast
  • Extra half cup of flour for kneading
    KNEADING TIME:(30 MINUTES)
  • 5 minutes in the bowl
  • 25 minutes on the table or flat surface (knead and beat)

Methodology

  1. In a bowl, mix the dry ingredients which is flour, sugar, salt, and yeast.
  2. Make a well in the center of the dried ingredients, add the egg,water and half of the butter. mix together in the bowl for 5 minutes until all ingredient are well incorporated.
  3. Transfer the dough to a floured clean surface, add the remaining butter and knead together for another 10 minutes.
  4. Now beat the dough against the table kneading as you go for 15 minutes until it becomes smooth,elastic and no more sticky to the table.
  5. Allow the dough to rest for 5 minutes before cutting with knife or scraper, measure on scale, 80gram each.
  6. Gently mould the dough with your hands, forming round shape, stitch and rob on the table to get smooth balls.
  7. Place them on a floured baking sheet, set aside in a warm place and allow to rise for about 1 hour 30 minutes.
  8. Heat the oil on a low heat, once hot fry the doughnut 3 minutes on each side until golden brown(must be on a low heat).
  9. Drain the doughnuts on paper towels.
  10. Sugar, fill or glaze the doughnuts as desired.
